01  THE NAME
================================================================================

    how far?

Lowercase. Spoken. Nigerian Pidgin for "what's up."

The name was chosen because every successful social network is spoken before
it is written. WhatsApp is literally "what's up." TikTok is a sound. Facebook
is two common words fused. BeReal is a sentence. None of them are abstractions.

THE NAME HAS THREE MEANINGS, AND TWO ARE THE PRODUCT:

    1. "How far?" = what's up?
       A greeting. The thing you say when you arrive somewhere.

    2. "How far down?"
       This is literally the core gesture of the app. The user drags down
       through a place's history. The name IS the interaction.

    3. "How far back?"
       How old is this place's memory? Every anniversary screen writes itself.

PASSED: two syllables, unmisspellable, works as a verb ("let me how-far this
corner"), already in people's mouths, rooted in the culture we are building
from rather than imported from California.

02  WHAT IT IS
================================================================================

ONE SENTENCE
    The ground remembers. You can only leave something where you've actually
    stood.

THE PITCH
    You walk up to a corner. You open the app. It shows you what other people
    left in the ground there -- voices, sounds, 15 seconds each. You can only
    add your own while you are physically standing on that spot. What you drop
    sinks. What everyone else dropped before you has sunk further, and you dig
    down to find it.

    Your city has a memory. It is made of everyone who was actually there.

FIVE PRIMITIVES -- change one and the product changes

    P1  PLACE IS THE ATOM
        The unit of organisation is a location, not a person. There are no
        profiles to follow. You carry places.

    P2  PRESENCE-GATED WRITE
        You can only add to a place while physically inside it. Verified
        on-device. Location is never transmitted, stored or shown.

    P3  DEPTH, NOT RECENCY
        Content is ordered by age-strata. Older = deeper = harder to reach,
        and more valuable when found.

    P4  PRESENCE IS A COUNT, NEVER A COORDINATE
        Liveness is an integer. "6 here right now." Nobody can ever see where
        you are. This is the line Zenly crossed and the line we do not cross.

    P5  LOCAL-FIRST
        A drop is written to device storage instantly and always succeeds.
        Sync is opportunistic. If the network fails, the product does not.

03  WHY THIS AND NOT ANOTHER FEED
================================================================================

We researched the failures. Every one died the same three ways.

    BeReal      Ritual without accumulation. Best mechanic in the industry,
                ~40% daily-usage decline, cloned by Instagram and TikTok in
                one quarter. A clever mechanic creates a spike; nothing
                compounds.
    Yik Yak     Anonymity -> toxicity -> school bans. No business model.
                Raised $73.5M, sold for $1M.
    Zenly       35M MAU, zero monetisation, killed by its parent.
    Recho,      "Drop a pin for strangers." Nobody cares about strangers'
    VoicePin,   pins. Empty on first open, so never opened twice.
    WhisprGeo
    Lapse,      The constraint IS the product. Constraints are copyable.
    Dispo
    Poparazzi   Great mechanic (you can only post photos OF friends, not of
                yourself). Slowed after fast start -- no retention hook.
    Cluster,    Useful utilities, not networks. No daily reason to open.
    Groupsy

THE THREE FAILURE MODES THIS ARCHITECTURE IS BUILT TO AVOID

    1. THE EMPTY ROOM
       Every failed app requires other people to already be there. Day one is
       empty, so day two never happens.
       OUR FIX: a place with nothing in it is not an empty state, it is an
                invitation. "Nothing here yet. You're the first."

    2. RITUAL WITHOUT ACCUMULATION
       OUR FIX: the strata accumulate. Every day the ground gets deeper. Year
                three of a place is uncatchable by a competitor.

    3. NO BUSINESS MODEL
       OUR FIX: high-intent local advertising -- the most valuable ad
                inventory in existence, and the one Meta is structurally
                worst at serving. A club's Saturday night IS its marketing.


================================================================================
04  THE CORE LOOP
================================================================================

    1. You walk into a place.
    2. The app (on-device geofence, low power) recognises you are here.
    3. It shows you the memory of this place -- voices left by people who
       stood here before you, ordered by depth.
    4. You hold to record. Fifteen seconds. Voice, ambient sound, whatever.
    5. You let go. It sinks into the ground with a visible ripple.
    6. It waits there for whoever stands next.

THE RETURN HOOK
    You walk past a place you have seeded and it glows differently for you.
    Someone added to it. Your mark grew.

THE EMOTIONAL PAYLOAD
    You just left something in the ground for a stranger, in the exact square
    metre where you stood.

05  THE STRATA ENGINE
================================================================================

Depth is DERIVED from age, never stored. depth_state is a materialised cache
of a pure function of age. This matters because the age thresholds will change
as we learn what feels right, and derived depth makes that a config change
rather than a migration.

    LAYER        AGE          COLOUR      STATE
    Surface      0 - 1 h      #FFF3D0     Loud, full colour. Default view on
                                          arrival.
    Fresh        1 - 24 h     #FFC24A     Muted but warm. Default view.
    Recent       1 - 7 d      #FF8A5B     Scroll within the place.
    Settling     7 - 30 d     #E2607E     Scroll.
    Sediment     1 - 6 mo     #A45BA8     DIG -- deliberate pull-down gesture.
    Deep         6 - 12 mo    #5B5BA8     Dig.
    Bedrock      1 y +        #3E4E60     Anniversaries + "on this day, here".

THE PRODUCT INSIGHT BAKED IN
    Sediment and bedrock are deliberately HARDER to reach. Friction is not a
    bug here -- it is what makes finding something old feel like discovery
    instead of scrolling.

    Getting older makes content DEEPER, not deader. Instagram archives (nobody
    visits). Snapchat deletes. Fade hard-deletes at 24h. FlashFeed deletes or
    extends. Nobody has built "older = more precious."

Reclassification runs as a background sweep every 15 minutes over only the
places touched in that window. Never scan the table.


================================================================================
06  SCREENS
================================================================================

There is no feed, no tabs, no profile grid. Every screen is a view of ground.
The only primary action in the entire app is HOLD TO DROP.

--- 01 THE GROUND ----------------------------------------------------------
    A living topographic surface, not a map with pins. Places are POOLS OF
    LIGHT, not markers. Size = volume of moments. Colour = how alive the
    surface is right now. No labels unless you zoom in.
    Your position is a single Halo point. It is never shared with anyone.
    Bottom: one "hold to drop" bar. Nothing else.

--- 02 A PLACE -------------------------------------------------------------
    A vertical cross-section of time. Each band is a stratum. Drag DOWN to
    dig. As you descend the palette cools and the overlapping voices get
    quieter and slower until it is almost geological.
    Bottom band is bedrock: "Two years ago, here -- 4 voices."

--- 03 DROP ----------------------------------------------------------------
    One gesture. Hold anywhere on the ring; it fills over 15 seconds.
    Release and the moment physically sinks with a ripple.
    Copy underneath never changes: "Let go and it sinks. It waits for
    whoever stands next."

--- 04 MY GROUND -----------------------------------------------------------
    Not a grid of your posts. A TERRAIN -- every place you have left something
    in forms a contour peak, coloured by how long ago you were last there.
    Stats: Places / Moments / Deepest. There is no follower number anywhere.

--- 05 LIVE ----------------------------------------------------------------
    "6 here right now." A count, never a coordinate. Below it, the place's own
    history ticking -- new surfaces appear at the top in surface-white and
    cool as they age downward.

        #FFB627   halo, primary action and the "you are here" dot
        #E08C12   halo deep, pressed

        Halo is the only saturated colour in the chrome and it means exactly
        one thing: someone is here, alive, right now. Nothing else may use it.

    STRATA SPECTRUM (hue = depth -- see section 05)
        Hot at the surface, cooling as you descend, like a thermal map or
        cooling iron. The colour is LOAD-BEARING: remove it and you remove
        the mechanic. Nobody can copy the look without copying the product.

        This is our "rainbow" -- and it is structural, not decorative.
        We rejected a decorative rainbow because owning a rainbow means
        owning nothing (Meta owns blue; you can say the word) and because
        in 2026 the multicolour gradient is the most overused visual
        signature in AI and crypto branding.

TYPE
        Wordmark   System sans, weight 700, lowercase, tight tracking
                   (-0.045em) on "how". The word "far" is set with each
                   letter progressively wider spaced (+0.10em, +0.24em) so
                   the word physically enacts distance as you read it.
                   Only the question mark is coloured, in Halo.
        UI face    System sans throughout.
        Serif      Appears only in place names and numerals that matter.

RADII
        Cards 12dp · Fields 10dp · Buttons 14dp · Bubbles 14dp
        Button height pinned at 52dp.

MOTION -- ONE RULE
        Every animation must be INFORMATION. Pulse = activity. Sink =
        committed. Dig sweep = depth. Nothing bounces for delight.
        No confetti, no like-explosions, no pull-to-refresh spinner.
        Restraint is what makes it feel expensive.

        The sink     Releasing a moment animates it downward into the ground
                     with a single ripple. Weight, gravity, finality.
        The dig      Dragging through strata sweeps light across each band
                     in sequence, top to bottom. You feel yourself going down.
        The pulse    Places breathe. A place being dropped into right now
                     pulses faster and brighter -- you can see life from a
                     distance without any notification.

VOICE -- THE COPY WRITES ITSELF
        Empty state   "Nothing here yet. You're the first."
        Dig prompt    "How far down?"
        Permission    "It only works where you are. Nothing stored, nothing

        After a drop  "Sunk. It waits for whoever stands next."
        Bedrock       "Two years down. Four voices."
        Anniversary   "One year ago, you stood here."
        Notification  "Someone answered you at Rumuokoro."
        Onboarding    "How far back does your city go?"

        NEVER SAY: "Welcome!" · "Connect with friends" · "Post" · "Like" ·
        "Follow" · "Trending" · "Discover". Every one of those belongs to the

================================================================================
08  WHAT THIS APP REFUSES
================================================================================

Each of these is a structural refusal, not an oversight. If one appears, the
product is broken.

    FOLLOWER COUNTS      You follow places, not people. No number is attached
                         to any human anywhere in the app.
    INFINITE SCROLL      Every surface has a bottom. Reach bedrock, you have
                         reached the end. On purpose.
    ALGORITHMIC FEED     Strict chronology, ordered by depth. Nothing ranks.
                         Nothing optimises for engagement.
    LIVE LOCATION        The brightest line. Presence is an integer, never a
                         coordinate. Zenly died of this.
    RED BADGES           Notifications only ever concern a place you have
                         left something in. Never social pressure.
    LIKES                You can add your own moment in reply. You cannot
                         rate someone else's.
    EDITING / FILTERS    Capture is final. No retakes. Lapse and The Roll
                         proved people want this.
    SHARING OUT          A moment belongs to the ground it was dropped in.
                         It does not travel.

09  TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

SHAPE

    Local-first is non-negotiable. The device store is the source of truth;
    the server is a replica. A drop never fails because of a network.
    Realtime is separated from the API because presence counts and surface
    updates have completely different scaling properties from CRUD.

    CLIENT (React Native / Expo)
      GPS + WiFi + BLE -> Presence Engine (on-device, nothing leaves)
                       -> Local Store (SQLite + files, source of truth)
                       -> Sync Engine (opportunistic)
                              |
                              | HTTPS / WS
              +---------------+---------------+
              v               v               v
        API (Go or       Realtime        Object Store
         Elixir)         (Channels)      (Cloudflare R2)
         places          presence counts  audio blobs
         moments         live surfaces    ~45KB each
         attestation                      immutable
              |               |
              v               v
        Postgres +       Redis
        PostGIS + H3     presence + pubsub

DATA MODEL

    place
        id, centroid (geography Point 4326), h3_cells, name (null until
        crowd-confirmed), moment_count, surface_count, presence_count
        (ephemeral, Redis, never persisted), steward_id, created_at

        PLACES ARE DISCOVERED, NOT DEFINED. They emerge from the density of
        moments. This matters because it means the product works in Lagos and
        Port Harcourt, where POI databases are terrible.
        Algorithm: H3 at resolution 12 (~307 m2). DBSCAN over moment
        centroids nightly. Merge cells sharing an edge above a density
        threshold. Recompute incrementally.

    moment
        id, place_id, author_id (pseudonymous, no public profile),
        point (rounded to ~5m), duration_s (max 15), audio_key, image_key
        (null), created_at, depth_state, attestation (jsonb),
        attestation_tier (0-4), parent_id (null), status

    Critical index: every read in the app is place -> moments ordered by
    created_at desc, filtered by depth band. That is one index.

PRESENCE VERIFICATION -- TIERED

    We are not overselling this. No consumer presence system is unspoofable.
    The goal is not cryptographic certainty, it is making faking ANNOYING.

    Tier 0   GPS geofence only. Beats nobody. Trivially spoofed.
    Tier 1   + WiFi BSSID set. Beats remote spoofing -- you must actually
             see the access points.
    Tier 2   + barometric/altitude. Adds vertical disambiguation.
    Tier 3   CO-PRESENCE ATTESTATION. BLE challenge/response with other
             devices in the same place; each signs the other's ephemeral
             token. Beats fake farms -- you need real bodies in the room.
             Modelled on the GAEN / Exposure Notification protocol:
             rotating ephemeral identifiers changing every ~15 min, derived
             from a daily key held only on-device. The server verifies
             internal consistency and time-adjacency, and CANNOT learn who
             was there or where.
    Tier 4   Venue QR / NFC for clubs, venues, events.

CO-PRESENCE ATTESTATION DOES DOUBLE DUTY
    The same BLE channel that proves you were there also carries offline mesh
    gossip. At a Port Harcourt concert with 5,000 phones and dead data,
    moments still spread device to device. One device that eventually reaches
    the network uploads the batch, signed by the originators.

    Instagram cannot function without a connection. At that concert, THE
    CROWD IS THE NETWORK. One primitive, two payoffs.

LIVELINESS

    Signal              Mechanism                              Target
    Presence count      Client heartbeat on entering a place    < 2 s
                        (local geofence trigger). Redis with
                        TTL. Broadcast as integer only.
    Surface ticker      New moment -> LISTEN/NOTIFY ->          < 1 s
                        fan-out to place subscribers.
    Place pulse         Client-side from surface velocity.       0
                        No round trip.
    Your places         Push when someone drops in a place      < 30 s
                        you have seeded. The ONLY push.

    The heartbeat sends place_id + a rotating token. It NEVER sends
    coordinates. No way to query who. No history retained. Counts expire.

    DELIBERATELY NOT LIVE: no typing indicators, no read receipts, no
    "X is online", no live location of any human, ever.

BUDGETS

    Audio: Opus, mono, 24 kbps, 15 s  =  ~45 KB
    Optional image: WebP q60, 720p    =  ~40 KB
    Metadata + attestation            =  < 1 KB
    Typical drop                      =  ~45 KB (audio only)

    One Instagram photo is 200-500 KB. One TikTok loop is 1-5 MB.
    A drop is roughly ONE TWENTIETH of a photo and ONE HUNDREDTH of a TikTok.
    At ~$0.50/GB in Nigeria, a hundred drops cost less than one TikTok
    scroll session.

    Session target: < 100 KB excluding drops. Pre-fetch the surface layer
    for the 20 places nearest your predicted route, on WiFi only.

    Infra at 100k MAU: Postgres+PostGIS ~$180 · Redis ~$40 · R2 storage ~$8
    · R2 egress $0 · Realtime ~$60 · Map tiles ~$20  =  ~$310/month.
    Zero-egress R2 is not a minor optimisation, it is a strategic choice.
    Egress is the line item that destroys social apps in emerging markets.

STACK

    Mobile     React Native via Expo -- Android-first, AND EAS Build produces
               the iOS binary in the cloud so no Mac is ever needed.
    Map        MapLibre GL + Protomaps (self-hosted tiles, flat cost).
               Google/Mapbox billing would be the largest cost in the company.
    Backend    Elixir/Phoenix preferred (PubSub + Presence are purpose-built
               for our liveliness model). Go + Fastify acceptable alternative.
    Database   Postgres + PostGIS + h3 extension.
    Cache      Redis (TTL'd counters, pubsub backplane).
    Storage    Cloudflare R2 (zero egress).
    Audio      Opus, mono, 24 kbps.
    Local DB   SQLite (expo-sqlite) + expo-file-system. Local-first requires
               a real local store, not AsyncStorage.

11  THE AFRICA CARD
================================================================================

This architecture fits the next billion in a way no incumbent can match.

    AUDIO-NATIVE      Voice notes are already the native format of African
                      mobile. ~1GB at $0.50 in Nigeria. Audio is an order of
                      magnitude cheaper than video.
    OFFLINE-TOLERANT  Pre-fetch the timelines of places you are heading to;
                      upload when you have signal. Every incumbent assumes
                      always-on broadband.
    ANDROID-FIRST     ~75%+ of downloads. Entry-level handsets are getting
                      MORE expensive (76% of monthly income for the poorest
                      20% in Sub-Saharan Africa, GSMA 2026), so small
                      payloads are not a nice-to-have, they are the product.
    CULTURALLY        "The story of a place, told by those who were there"
      NATIVE          is the village square and the oral tradition. It is not
                      imported Silicon Valley software wearing an African
                      flag. The name itself is Pidgin.

12  MARKET CONTEXT (researched, September 2026)
================================================================================

    Gen Z is pruning: ~32% deleted a social app in the last 12 months; the
    average number of platforms adults use is DECLINING.
    The winners are small, participatory, niche: Partiful, Geneva, Fizz,
    Beli, Corner.

    PRSN launched a motion-verified human-only network (June 2026).
    We get authenticity by ARCHITECTURE instead of biometrics.

    battery. India tried to ban all three in July 2026.
    Moltbook, a social network for AI agents, was acquired by Meta in
    March 2026. The dead-internet problem is real and getting worse.

13  BUILD SEQUENCE
================================================================================

Not a schedule -- a dependency order. Each phase ends with something you can
put in a human's hand.

    PHASE 0  FAKE IT
             A clickable prototype. No backend.
             Answers: does the dig gesture feel good? Do people pull down
             without being told to?

    PHASE 1  PROOF OF PRESENCE
             Two phones. Prove tier-1 and tier-3 attestation works, in a real
             location, with GPS spoofing attempted.
             Answers: can we actually gate this?

    PHASE 2  ONE PLACE, REAL
             Full drop -> sink -> strata -> playback loop, server-backed, one
             physical location seeded by hand.

    PHASE 3  THE GROUND
             Map surface, clustering, glow, offline store-and-forward.

    PHASE 4  LIVENESS
             Presence counts, surface ticker, your-places notifications.

    PHASE 5  MESH
             BLE gossip. Test at a real event with dead signal.

    PHASE 6  MODERATION
             Place stewards, quarantine, reporting. REQUIRED before any real
             launch. Yik Yak skipped this and died.

    DO NOT START AT PHASE 3. The temptation is to build the map because it is
    the impressive part. The map is worthless if presence-gating does not hold.

15  THE ONLY TEST THAT MATTERS
================================================================================

Everything else is engineering. The single risk that matters is whether
strangers will leave something in the ground for other strangers.

THE RUMUOKORO JUNCTION TEST -- zero code required

    1. Print a sign. Put it at one busy Port Harcourt location. It says:
           "Leave 15 seconds for the next person who stands here.
            Send a voice note to this WhatsApp number."
    2. Run it for one week.
    3. Count submissions. Ask each person one question: would you come back
       to hear what others left?

    PASS    50 people drop in a week and 30 say they would return.
            Then this is real.
    FAIL    3 people drop. No amount of engineering saves it -- and you will
            have spent a week instead of a year finding that out.
